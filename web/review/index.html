How the review works

Data. Opening a .dem runs the viewer parse plus a review pass: every player_hurt, every shot with pitch and velocity, and tick-level samples (every 2 ticks) of all players for 3 s before each hit and kill, including the server's spotted-by mask. Exported .strats replays lack this detail and get a reduced review (no ADR, reaction or crosshair placement).

Mechanics. Crosshair placement is the angle from your view to the enemy's head when the server first marks them spotted by you; reaction is spot → first shot; time-to-damage is spot → first hit. Counter-strafing compares speed at each duel shot with an accurate-speed threshold. Spotting is radar data and can lag true visibility, so values read optimistic.

Decisions. Heuristics flag dry opening deaths, isolated or untraded deaths, missed trades (with line of sight when spotting is available), re-peeks while damaged, exposure to multiple enemies, unused utility, team flashes and low-percentage fights that should have been saves. Highlights cover trades, flash assists, multi-kills and clutches.

Ratings. HLTV 1.0 uses the public formula; HLTV 2.0 is a community approximation; Impact is win-probability added per round (each kill, plant and defuse valued by a heuristic win-probability model, shared by damage). Scope Rating blends rating, impact, mechanics and decisions around 1.00. None are official HLTV, Leetify or FACEIT numbers.
