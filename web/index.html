Made for the next read.

Open an uncompressed CS2 .dem file. Parsing happens in a background worker on your device. You can also import an exported .strats replay.

The essentials

  • Click a round to jump. Use Pistol strats to play R1 followed by the detected second-half pistol, then stop.
  • Focus a team to keep tracking that roster after the side switch. Click a player to emphasize their movement and utility.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset. Use the floor selector on Nuke, Vertigo and Train.
  • Click a utility event to replay its flight and detonation. The timeline and all effects support backwards seeking.

Accuracy

Player positions, facing, grenade flight, effect centers and available expiry events come from the demo. Smoke and fire areas are approximate footprints: they do not model wall clipping, flame cells or temporary smoke holes. A ~ marks an estimated lifetime when an expiry event is missing.

Bundled map images use matching overview coordinates. Older demos can contain a different map revision. You can import a matching radar and its coordinates below; unsupported maps are never silently shown as a different map.

Use a custom / historical radar

Use the values from that map version's overview file. Coordinates refer to a 1024-unit radar square. Saved in exported replays.
